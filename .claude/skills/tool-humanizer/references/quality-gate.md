# Quality Gate — Final Self-Check

Run against the revised draft before returning output (Step 8). If any item fails, fix the draft and re-run the whole checklist — don't spot-fix and skip the rest.

## Voice Preservation

- [ ] Does the revised draft still sound like the same author who wrote the original — checked against the 3-5 traits noted in Step 3?
- [ ] Did a distinctive sentence get flattened just to make it "tidier," even though it had no actual AI pattern?
- [ ] Is sentence length still varied, or did every sentence get smoothed to the same length?
- [ ] Did genuine hedges ("I think," "maybe," "to be honest") survive where they expressed real uncertainty or the writer's spoken rhythm, rather than getting cut on reflex?

## Meaning & Specificity

- [ ] Did you invent, add, or embellish any claim, example, stat, or opinion the original didn't have?
- [ ] Did a concrete detail (a number, a name, a specific mechanism) get smoothed into something vague? ("cut deploy time from 40 to 4 minutes" should never become "improved efficiency")
- [ ] Are all quotes, data points, and attributed statements unchanged from the original?

## Residual Patterns

- [ ] Any banned words or empty phrases from `pattern-library.md` still present?
- [ ] Any formatting slop left — emoji in headings, mid-sentence bold, bullets where two sentences of prose would read better, headers over two-sentence sections?
- [ ] Does the ending restate the piece ("In conclusion...") or land on a fake-profound kicker line, instead of the clearest concrete point already in the draft?

## Final Read

- [ ] Read it once as the reader, not the editor — does it sound like a person talking?
- [ ] If you stumble on any sentence, rewrite that sentence and re-run this checklist from the top.
