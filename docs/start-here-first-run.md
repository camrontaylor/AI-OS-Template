# Set up AI-OS for your business

Open the AI-OS folder in your agent and say **“Help me set up AI-OS.”**
Claude Code also supports `/start-here`. Both use `.claude/commands/start-here.md`.

Bring a short description of your business, who you help, why people choose you,
and a few examples of writing you like. A website, brand guide or approved email can help.
The agent asks focused questions, reuses answers and existing files, and builds:

- Your working preferences in `context/USER.md`.
- Your voice and examples in `brand_context/voice-profile.md` and `samples.md`.
- Your offer and angle in `brand_context/positioning.md`.
- Your ideal customer in `brand_context/icp.md`.

It then helps you choose skills and recommends a first task. Setup is optional:
you can ask for work immediately, even with a blank profile.
If your brand files are already populated, ask for a specific update rather than rebuilding them.
Private backup and connected apps are separate choices, not prerequisites for onboarding.

Try: **“Draft an introduction using my brand context. Save it and flag any unsupported claims.”**
